Instructions

Welcome to the rabbit study tool!
There are a few basic rules to the tool.

Navigation

Each of the pages are connected to the others they relate to. The homepage is connected to all pages, so if you get lost, just go back there.

Pages

There are six pages: the instructions page (here), the homepage, a tasks page, a shop page, an interaction page, and a timer page. Each give you different forms of interaction.
The homepage allows customisation of the rabbit's name, and shows a coin count and buttons to all other pages.
In the tasks page, you will be able to input [3] tasks, and then head over to the adjustable timer page to complete the study tasks to get coins.
These coins can be spent on buying food from the shop page - food that is bought goes to the interact page. The shop page has limited and ordinary items, each having different effects. Limited aren't there for long, so make sure to grab them when you can!
The timer page has an adjustable pomodoro timer - you can put in anywhere from 5 minutes to an hour of solid study for a session, and your break time will automatically adjust. The longer you study, the more coins and happiness you get!
The final page is the interact page - you can feed your rabbit with what you bought, increasing their happiness, and also clean them and pat them for minor happiness boosts.

Warnings

If you let your rabbits happiness drop too much, they can become sick. Remember to study often to prevent this!

Other information

You can rename your rabbit in the text box above its head on the homepage!
